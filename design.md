# Fieldnotes design system

## Direction

Newsprint, adapted for an engineering mathematics reference: warm paper, ink-first type, thin rules, and a restrained brick accent. The layout remains purpose-specific rather than imitating a newspaper page.

## Type and color

- Zilla Slab 700 for display headings and the wordmark.
- Spectral 400/500 for body copy, navigation, labels, and controls.
- Libre Franklin 500 only for numeric figures that benefit from tabular alignment.
- Light mode uses pale warm paper, dark ink, muted rules, and dried-brick links.
- Dark mode keeps the same roles with warm charcoal paper and reversed ink.
- Polygon diagrams use brick for the highlighted side, a cool neutral for the diagonal, and sepia for the apothem; labels remain explicit.

## Page composition

- Home: a typographic lead, a mathematical figure with caption, and ruled pathways.
- Formula index: searchable controls followed by dense, ruled reference entries.
- Lesson: simulation first, then outcomes, explanation, vocabulary, comparison, and sources.
- Practice: a focused work area beside the answer and progress controls.

## Interaction and accessibility

Keep the theme toggle, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and accessible KaTeX MathML. Use rectangular controls and rules rather than cards, shadows, or rounded containers. State and diagram colors are always accompanied by text or line-style differences.
