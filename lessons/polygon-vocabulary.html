    Polygon vocabulary.

    Not every closed-looking shape is a polygon.

    A triangle is a simple polygon. It is closed, has three straight sides, and no sides cross.

    A convex pentagon is a simple polygon. Each interior angle is less than 180 degrees.

    A concave pentagon is still a simple polygon. At least one interior angle is greater than 180 degrees.

    An open chain is not a polygon. Its segments do not form a closed boundary.

    A curved boundary is not a polygon. A polygon is made only from straight segments.

    This self-crossing figure is not a simple polygon. Nonadjacent sides cross.

    Equal sides are not the whole story.

    A rhombus has equal side lengths but need not have equal angles. A rectangle has equal angles but need not have equal side lengths. A polygon is regular only when it is both equilateral and equiangular.
